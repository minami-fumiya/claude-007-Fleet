# Roadmap

> バージョン番号 (アルファ版のくくり) は `docs/VERSIONS.md` を正とする。下の「v1.0」などの表記は旧来の呼び方。

## Phase 1: Foundation (Sprints 0–2)
- Sprint 0: Environment & infrastructure setup
- Sprint 1: Type system, scene base, Yamato playable
- Sprint 2: Combat system, HUD, basic enemy AI

## Phase 2: Content (Sprints 3–5)
- Sprint 3: Full IJN roster, FleetBuilder, formations
- Sprint 4: USN, Kriegsmarine, AI v2, submarine warfare
- Sprint 5: Royal Navy, customization, captains, aircraft

## Phase 3: Systems & Polish (Sprints 6–7)
- Sprint 6: Home base, docks, resources, research tree
- Sprint 7: Audio, effects, weather, night battle → v1.0 tag (implemented; tag after merging to main)

## Phase 4: Advanced (Sprints 8–10+)
- Sprint 8: Campaign map, convoy ops, shore bombardment, FogOfWar
- Sprint 9: Historical scenarios, refit system, encyclopedia
- Sprint 10: Fictional ships, more scenarios and a third theater, service record and medals (implemented)
- Sprint 11: Polish — settings (volume), help, save export / import / reset (proposed and implemented)
- Sprint 12: Playtest feedback (#15–#30) — nation bases, growth from one destroyer, 7 navies, research / refit expansion, square map (implemented)
- Sprint 13: Playtest feedback rounds 2–3 (#32, #75) → 0.0.8–0.0.22-alpha (see docs/VERSIONS.md)
- Later: Expansion — open-world free mode and the rest of #63 → 0.0.23-alpha and later; 3D battle view (#96) closes the alpha
- Later: Multiplayer (TBD — needs a server)
